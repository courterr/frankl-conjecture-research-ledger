## Correction to the previous handoff

The previous APPROACH-LEDGER.md summarized mathematical routes and results. It
did not establish when each route was proposed, what literature was checked
before investment, or why a decision to continue was made. It cannot support
the claim that the agreed novelty gate was consistently enforced.

The user's workflow was: generate an apparently new approach, check whether
it appears in the literature, then attempt it if no match is found. A limited
negative search can support provisional exploration, but cannot prove novelty.
This ledger must not manufacture contemporaneous checks from a later search.

## What can be counted honestly?

- Four overlapping broad established method families are identified here.
- Six more specific question/result entries are tracked separately.
- No exact count of historical redundant approaches, wasted sessions, or
  successful novelty-gate decisions can be inferred from this ledger.
- No specific result is certified historically new. A proof/checker pass is
  evidence about correctness of its stated scope, not priority.

Using an established method is not itself wasted work. Repeating an already
known special result might still produce a useful example, but must not be
marketed as a novel approach. Determining redundancy requires comparing the
precise claim with an earlier equivalent or stronger result.

## Required record for future substantial attempts

For each candidate, record these fields BEFORE a substantial attempt:

1. Precise mathematical claim and how it differs from known machinery.
2. Closest sources, sections/theorems, search terms and search date.
3. Status: established/equivalent; known method with potentially different
   application; no exact match located; unresolved; or not checked.
4. Decision and reason: stop novelty claim, retain as cited tool, do bounded
   exploration, or seek expert assessment. Record the decision's actual date.
5. Afterwards: exact result, failure or inconclusive outcome, evidence and scope.

A “no exact match located” result must remain provisional. An undocumented
historical check stays undocumented unless a dated source record is recovered.
Do not rewrite a retrospective assessment as a pre-attempt clearance.
