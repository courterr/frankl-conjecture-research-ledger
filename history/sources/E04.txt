PRE-SEARCH CANDIDATE
Seed P022: temporal irreversibility and the hidden cost of recovering a vanished state. Translation: reverse the union operation probabilistically rather than transfer abundance between spatial fibres.
Candidate object: fractional convolution roots of the uniform distribution on a finite union semilattice F (assume empty in F). For A in F define C(A)=#{B in F:B subset A}/|F|. For t in (0,1), Mobius-invert C(A)^t on the inclusion poset to obtain signed weights mu_t(A). Then mu_t has total mass1 and mu_t convolved with itself under union has cumulative C^(2t). Question: which F have mu_t>=0 for every t>0, and does this restricted class admit a usable abundant-element argument? More exploratory: can the first negative coefficient or negative mass provide a useful obstruction? These latter questions are not claims or established implications for Frankl.
This is materially different from triple-fibre/core-transfer bookkeeping, but could be an existing probability-on-semilattices framework. Search before treating it as new. There is no assertion that a numerical statistic alone proves the conjecture.


MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATION
Assume empty belongs to the finite union-closed family F, with m members. For A in F define
 C(A)=#{B in F:B subset A}/m.
For t>0 define coefficients mu_t recursively along inclusion:
 mu_t(A)=C(A)^t-sum over B proper subset A, B in F of mu_t(B).
This is finite-poset Mobius inversion. The coefficients sum to1. For independent unions the cumulative probabilities multiply, because a union lies inside A exactly when each operand lies inside A. Thus the formal union convolution satisfies mu_s vee mu_t=mu_(s+t).

The crucial missing condition is positivity. Without mu_t(A)>=0 for every A, this is a signed measure rather than a possible random experiment. Infinite divisibility asks for probability roots of all integer orders; in this finite positive-C setting those roots, when they exist, are uniquely specified by the displayed cumulative powers. Any candidate root cannot put mass on a set outside F: repeating that same set in every independent draw would give it positive target mass, contrary to the support of the uniform law. This supports using the inclusion poset of F itself.

SMALL DIAGNOSTIC: POSITIVITY IS A REAL OBSTRUCTION
As a sanity check on the proposed object, take
 F={empty,{a,b},{a,c},{b,c},{a,b,c}}.
This family is union-closed and all coordinates occur in3 of5 members, so Frankl holds.
For t=1/2 the unique signed root has masses
 empty:1/sqrt(5),
 each pair:(sqrt(2)-1)/sqrt(5),
 top:1+(2-3sqrt(2))/sqrt(5)<0.
The top coefficient is approximately -0.0029394051. Its negativity is exact:3sqrt(2)>2+sqrt(5), which follows by squaring and using81>80. Direct union convolution of these signed masses returns the uniform1/5 law to floating-point tolerance.

Thus an elementary union-closed family with abundant elements can already fail to have a probabilistic square root. Negative root mass alone is not a certificate that Frankl fails. This diagnostic characterizes a potential failure of the proposed mechanism; it is not an attempt to claim an old framework as new mathematics or a full proof for the divisible subclass.
