QUESTION AND MATHEMATICAL FRAMEWORK
F is finite and union-closed, containing the empty set. q_F(T) counts ordered union pairs producing T, r_F(T) counts members contained in T, and e_F(T)=(q_F(T)-2r_F(T)+1)/2 counts unordered proper union decompositions. The underlying unresolved candidate says every positive global e-maximizer A contains a coordinate occurring in at least half of F.

For an internal core G=F intersect2^A, an exterior trace yields a nonempty, union-closed, G-stable trace family H containing A. If Q(X,Y) counts ordered pairs whose union is A, define C_G(H)=2Q(G,H)+Q(H,H). Global e-modal maximality gives the necessary cap
 C_G(H)-2|H|<=q_G(A).
A local weighting certificate uses nonnegative normalized weights w with
 sum_i w_i*(2*f_K(i)-|K|)>=0
for K=G and every permitted H. Summing the actual fibres gives abundance in the support. An inability to find such a weighting is not automatically a global counterexample.

All targets in this pilot have seven coordinates, A=127. For a support pair(a,b), write weights(p,1-p). Each family contributes
 s_b+(s_a-s_b)*p>=0, where s_i=2*f(i)-|K|.
Intersecting exact rational intervals over0<=p<=1 determines feasibility on that pair. Endpoints include all single-coordinate possibilities. Therefore blocking all21 support pairs would also rule out every unit-coordinate certificate.

For each completed baseline having blocked pairs, build a deterministic witness bank from the fibre rows used in exact support-infeasibility proofs, plus small negative-unit witnesses. A core row is NOT a fibre to preserve; it is updated after every core modification.

Candidate cores adjoin one trace and take union closure. A witness-preserving candidate must retain the bank's G stability, retain its cost admissibility, and keep every previously blocked support exactly infeasible using the bank plus the new core row. Candidate ranking maximizes minimum bank cost slack, then prefers smaller cores and lower trace indices. At most four preserving candidates and four other candidates are enumerated per round. The other additions provide exploratory context; they are not a randomized comparison group.

Continue at most two rounds, only on a strict superset of blocked pairs with no restored prior support. Choose the largest blocked count, then smaller core, then trace index. Incomplete cases cannot establish newly caused gains or surviving full-class certificates. Stop a lane on no monotone progress, no starting obstruction, or budgets. The primary cardinality bound is |H|<=floor((q_G-2|G|+3)/2). Adjusted cost is monotone on families containing A: each new trace adds at least2 cross-pair contributions with core A and at least2 ordered H-H contributions with A, minus2 for its cardinality. Thus cost pruning is sound. The independent root adjusted cost is2|G|-1 and each insertion subtracts2. Both implementations use the new adjusted cap, not the older q-mode cap.

FEASIBILITY VERDICT AND LIMITS
This session establishes two things: the witness-preserving mechanism can accumulate new support obstructions in at least one concrete lane, and the frozen short search does not show that it does so reliably across different seeds. The goal of defeating every support of size one or two was not met. We do not know whether that goal is achievable with this recipe, or whether a universal two-coordinate certificate theorem obstructs it in this domain.

The one-lane result is enough for a focused follow-up, not for a broad efficacy claim. The fixed-bank stability condition excludes most candidate additions, and the search examined only four preserving choices per round. Failure to improve a lane cannot be interpreted as impossibility, while the two sequential gains cannot be extrapolated into inevitable completion. The controls with no starting obstruction are not counted as failed attempts to extend an existing bank; the incomplete control is not counted as a clean negative.

The larger project still has no demonstrated general poetry advantage, no general abundance proof, and no historical novelty finding from this session. No literature check was performed here. All conclusions concern the explicitly defined local-certificate construction and the archived finite data.
