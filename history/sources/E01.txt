ORDINARY BASELINE, RECORDED BEFORE THE DRAW
Ordinary baseline expansion, before viewing a new poem. 2026-10-21.
Scope: eight explicit next directions; this is an operational stopping point, not exhaustive saturation. Earlier baseline and literature correction retained.
1. Charge negative triple fibres to their union fibre. Failure: many lower fibres share one target; sharp 4/9 construction realizes collision. Need a quantitative capacity invariant, not mere target existence.
2. Use the actual disjoint-set anchor Y and its forced full cube if S is deficient. Gap: cube itself is balanced and may be tiny relative to the family. No general outside-frequency bridge.
3. Delete an outside coordinate and induct. Gap: projected sets carry multiplicity 1 or 2; ordinary union-closed induction does not transfer automatically.
4. Find a normalized nonnegative weight vector making every fibre locally half-heavy. Failure: F34 forbids this certificate. Global weighting/LP still possible but established territory.
5. Relax inclusion matching using bounded downward moves. Gap: arbitrary injections restate Frankl, and universal monotone matching is already false in Edgington 2016. Need independently tractable restrictions on violations.
6. Reuse forced cube as many translation targets. Maps A to A union R collide; partition by outside trace resolves some collisions but gives only local inequalities already bounded by 4/9.
7. Use entropy on a coupling whose union has exactly the original law. Failure for fixed finite universe: containment and equal expected size force identical sets. Approximate laws need controlled transport costs; no quantitative improvement here.
8. Count incidences of outside coordinates in negative fibres. Sharp examples concentrate such incidences, but arbitrary negative-fibre supports need not be uniform or disjoint. A restricted theorem depending on anchor size or overlap could be tractable.
Working gap: translate structural necessity of an actual full cube into an outside-coordinate abundance certificate, without universal inclusion matching.

CANDIDATE AS RECORDED BEFORE SEARCH
Candidate E01: localization of triple deficiency around the union of disjoint members.
Let S in F have size 3, Y=union{A in F:A disjoint S}, E nonempty. If each x in S has f(x)<m/2, claim strictly more than 9m/16 members of F are contained in S union Y.
Poetry transformation: notice structurally absent traces rather than only the patterns present; treat regions without empty traces separately. Outside Y, empty traces are forbidden by definition, so low-trace deficits cannot live there.
Preliminary derivation: D(H)>=-|H|/3 for all top-containing triple fibres; if empty trace absent, D(H)>=3|H|/7. Let I count members contained in S union Y and O=m-I. Then total D >= -I/3+3O/7. Total D<0 implies I>9m/16.
Potential payoff: localize a deficient chosen triple's obstruction inside a majority subfamily, which itself is union-closed. Gap: abundance inside that restricted family may still be diluted below m/2 globally. Sharpness and literature status unchecked at this point.

MATHEMATICAL RESULT — COMPLETE PROOF
Let F be a finite union-closed family, m=|F|, and S in F with |S|=3. Let f(x) count members containing x. Define
  Y = union of all A in F with A intersect S empty,
with union of an empty collection interpreted as empty. Define
  I = number of A in F with A subset S union Y,
  O = m-I.
Then
  max over x in S of f(x) >= (36m-8I)/63.                 (1)
In particular:
  I <= 9m/16 implies some x in S has f(x)>=m/2.        (2)
Equivalently, if every x in S is below half, then I>9m/16. In fact the latter localization follows under the weaker assumption sum over S of f(x)<3m/2.

Proof. Partition F into nonempty fibres indexed by T subset U minus S:
  H_T = {R subset S : T union R in F}.
Each fibre is union-closed and contains S, because S is a member of F. Write h=|H_T| and
  D(H_T) = sum over R in H_T of (2|R|-3).
For a fibre, let e indicate presence of the empty trace, s count singleton traces, and p count pair traces. Since the top S is present,
  h=1+e+s+p,   D=3-3e-s+p,
and closure implies p>=binomial(s,2).

For every fibre, D>=-h/3. Indeed,
  3D+h=10-8e-2s+4p>=0.
For e=0 this is positive; for e=1 check s=0,1,2,3 using p>=binomial(s,2).

For a fibre with e=0, D>=3h/7. Indeed,
  7D-3h=18-10s+4p>=0.
The lower bounds for s=0,1,2,3 are 18,8,2,0.

If T is not a subset of Y, its fibre has no empty trace: otherwise T itself belongs to F and is disjoint from S, contradicting the definition of Y. Thus fibres based inside Y contribute at least -I/3 in total balance, while those based outside contribute at least 3O/7. Consequently,
  2 sum over x in S of f(x)-3m
      = sum_T D(H_T)
      >= -I/3 + 3O/7
      = (9m-16I)/21.
Rearranging and taking the largest of the three frequencies proves (1), and then (2). No unproved conjecture or computational assumption enters this argument.

ASYMPTOTIC SHARPNESS — COMPLETE CONSTRUCTION
For integer q>=3, take S={a,b,c}, and disjoint X={x_1,...,x_(3q)}. Assign labels l_i in S with exactly q indices for each label. Put T_i=X minus {x_i}.
Start with
  {S},
  {T_i, T_i union {l_i}, T_i union S} for every i,
  {X union R : R subset S}.
There are 9q+9 distinct members. Every element of S occurs 4q+5 times.

Add new points z_1,...,z_(q-2). For j=1,...,q-2 put Z_j={z_1,...,z_j}, and append the seven members
  X union Z_j union R, for all nonempty R subset S.
The resulting family is union-closed. Two different lower bases T_i union T_k give X. Appended bases form a nested chain and already contain X; their nonempty traces remain nonempty under every mixed union. All other unions remain in the specified layers.

The union of members disjoint from S is exactly Y=X. Every appended member is outside S union Y. Counts are
  m=16q-5,
  I=9q+9,
  f(a)=f(b)=f(c)=8q-3=(m-1)/2.
Hence all three elements are deficient and
  I/m=(9q+9)/(16q-5) tends to 9/16 from above.
No constant greater than 9/16 can universally replace the coefficient in the localization conclusion I>c*m. Equivalently, the leakage threshold 7/16 cannot be reduced by a fixed positive amount. The affine bound (1) is approached to within a constant independent of q.

The original X coordinates are highly abundant in this construction. It does not threaten Frankl's conjecture.

ATTEMPTED APPLICATION AND PRECISE STOPPING POINT
The result proves a genuine restricted case: if at least 7/16 of the members extend outside S union Y, some element of S is abundant. This condition is computable directly from F and the chosen member S.

When S is deficient, define G={A in F:A subset S union Y}. It is union-closed and contains more than 9m/16 members. Try to use its concentration to locate a globally abundant element in Y. This attempt stops at a dilution gap: even if some y has f_G(y)>=|G|/2, that does not imply f_F(y)>=m/2. Let H=F minus G. A successful lift needs an actual certificate
  (2f_G(y)-|G|)+(2f_H(y)-|H|)>=0
for one common y. The localization inequality supplies no such certificate. Assuming Frankl for G is itself only justified in an induction setup or a separately solved restricted case. Thus no global conclusion is claimed.

Sharpness rules out simply improving the localization constant indefinitely. A next substantive route must exploit cross-union compatibility between G and H, or find a different invariant. Mere existence of a majority subfamily is insufficient to complete our argument.

VALIDATION
The proof is primary; finite checks supplement it. The checker independently enumerated all 90 nonempty union-closed triple fibres containing the top and checked both local inequalities. It checked the construction for q=3,4,6,10,20, including closure, exact frequencies, anchor, and concentration.

It also enumerated all 4,959 nonempty labelled union-closed families on four available ground points and generated 3,000 deterministic random closures on five to eight available ground points. Neither set contained a deficient member-triple, so those broad checks did not exercise the conditional conclusion. They are not counted as evidence from thousands of successful deficient cases. The five explicit sharp constructions do exercise it.
