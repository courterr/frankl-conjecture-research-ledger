The general proper-decomposition abundance claim remains unresolved. No historical priority or causal benefit from poetry is established. No new poem was drawn; this is adversarial follow-through on the current poetry-derived branch.

MATHEMATICAL PROBLEM
Let F be a finite union-closed family containing the empty set. Let q_F(T) count ordered pairs of members whose union is T, and r_F(T) count members contained in T. Define
 e_F(T)=(q_F(T)-2r_F(T)+1)/2.
This is the number of unordered proper union decompositions of T. The candidate is that every positive global maximizer A of e_F, including a tied maximizer, contains a coordinate appearing in at least half the members of F.

For a six-coordinate target A=63, its internal core is G=F intersect2^A. Each occurring nonempty exterior trace Z gives H_Z={B intersect A:B in F, B outside A=Z}. Every H_Z is nonempty, contains A, is union-closed, and is G-stable under union. Write Q(X,Y) for the number of ordered pairs with union A and C_G(H)=2Q(G,H)+Q(H,H). Global e-modal maximality forces
 C_G(H)-2|H|<=q_G(A).
This adjusted cap, rather than the old stricter C_G(H)<=q_G(A), was used throughout the new enumeration.

The cap follows by counting proper union decompositions of D=A union Z inside the core-plus-one-fibre subfamily. It already supplies [C_G(H)-2(|G|+|H|)+1]/2 decompositions. Full F can add more, while e_F(A)=(q_G(A)-2|G|+1)/2 must dominate e_F(D).

A local weighting certificate is a normalized vector w>=0 with
 sum_i w_i*(2*f_K(i)-|K|)>=0
for K=G and for every permitted H. Summing over the actual fibres proves at least one coordinate in the support globally abundant. Permitted fibres may be individually possible without being jointly realizable; the certificate is valid because it handles a superset of all actual fibres.

An equal pair i,j has the particularly transparent condition
 f_K(i)+f_K(j)>=|K|.
Summing gives f_F(i)+f_F(j)>=|F|, hence at least one globally abundant coordinate. For some tested cores the combined core surplus is zero, so this report does not claim a uniform strict margin or uniqueness of the abundant coordinate.

EXACT PAIR FEASIBILITY, WITHOUT NUMERICAL LP DEPENDENCE
For a candidate pair(a,b), normalized weights are(p,1-p), 0<=p<=1. Each family K contributes the inequality
 s_b(K)+(s_a(K)-s_b(K))*p>=0,
where s_i(K)=2*f_K(i)-|K|. Intersecting the resulting rational intervals exactly determines pair feasibility. Endpoint bounds and returned weights use Python Fraction. Unit weights were tried first. The code contains a full-weight linear-program fallback only for a case where every pair fails; that branch was never reached.

Initial two-coordinate outputs included530 equal pairs,149 choices with ratios1:7, and3 with ratios1:5. The unequal outputs do NOT establish that unequal weights were necessary: they were merely the first interval endpoints found. Later audit found an equal pair for every one of these cases, possibly a different pair.

AUDIT AND INDEPENDENT CHECKS
All1,533 recorded certificate vectors were independently checked against their core and every stored fibre using exact rational arithmetic and a different bit-count implementation of coordinate frequencies. All463,842 stored fibre occurrences passed those weight inequalities. Every core was checked for union closure, distinct members, empty/top membership, and positive internally maximal e score.

Twelve cases were selected for independent whole-class enumeration: the eight largest fibre classes among cases needing two coordinates, all three cases whose initial chosen weights included1/6, and the first unit/pair examples from both archive and mutation sources, deduplicating the selection. This selected12 cases. The selection was saved before running the independent enumerator.

For those12, a Python include/exclude traversal with iterative union closure reproduced the entire fibre set of the primary C++ canonical incremental-closure enumerator. Total9,651 fibre occurrences and100,132 independent nodes; exact set equality in every selected case. All emitted fibres in those cases were also checked explicitly for top membership, union closure, G stability, and adjusted cost.

Independent whole-class enumeration was NOT run for the other1,521 cases. Their coverage rests on the completed primary canonical enumeration; their certificate arithmetic was checked against all returned fibres. The distinction between full-set independent checks and certificate arithmetic audit is intentional. This is computer-assisted work, not formal proof-assistant verification or external peer review.

The pruning bound is monotone: adding a trace h to a family containing A creates at least2 cross-pair contributions with g=A and at least2 ordered H-H contributions with A, while subtracting2 for the new trace. Thus C(H)-2|H| increases by at least2 and cannot return under the cap after pruning. The primary closure formula uses union closure of both G and the current H; the independent algorithm reaches closure by repeated binary unions instead.

EXPLORATORY STRUCTURAL RULE
The previous 'every pair works' hypothesis was already refuted in Exploration21 and is not revived here. Instead, every tested core has at least one equal pair certificate. In all tested cases, the pair of most frequent internal coordinates chosen by fixed tie-breaking is one such certificate. This is a more specific, falsifiable target for mathematical explanation or a further adversarial test.
The tie check is stronger within this dataset: every pair maximizing the sum of its two internal coordinate frequencies also certified every permitted fibre. No tied maximum-frequency pair failed. This too was a post-search exploratory check, not a general theorem.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CHANGE
The intended all-pair-support obstruction was not found. We should not claim to have beaten it, or interpret the search as a proof that it cannot exist. We have widened the tested collection substantially, obtained exact certificates for all searched cases, and exposed a crisp selection rule worth trying to prove or falsify.

The main conjecture and the existence of a universal local weighting remain open in this project. Even an exact failure of every local weighting would first be a limitation of this fibre proof strategy; it would not automatically be a global counterexample. Conversely, survival of this targeted search says nothing reliable about the probability of survival on unsearched cores or larger targets.

NEXT COURSE
A productive fork is to attack the internally most-frequent-pair rule structurally, or test it on larger targets with strict resource limits. Repeating nearby six-coordinate samples indefinitely would add less than identifying why the rule works here or constructing a genuinely different obstruction. Fresh target generation should be frozen before evaluation. Poetry attribution and historical novelty require their own comparisons and literature work.
