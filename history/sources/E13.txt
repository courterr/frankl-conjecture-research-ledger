MATHEMATICAL SETUP AND CERTIFICATE

F is a finite union-closed family of distinct sets containing empty.
q_F(A) counts ORDERED pairs (B,C) in F squared whose union is A, equal pairs
included. Fix a modal target A, of size n. G consists of the members inside A.
For a nonempty occurring exterior trace E, H={B intersect A: B minus A=E}.
Then H contains A, is union-closed, and satisfies G union H subset H, where
this means all pairwise unions g union h belong to H.

Write Q(K,L)=#{(k,l) in K times L:k union l=A}, and q=Q(G,G).
Every actual exterior fibre obeys
                 2Q(G,H)+Q(H,H) <= q.                        (1)
These are disjoint pair contributions to q_F(A union E), whereas q_F(A)=q.

Given nonnegative integer weights p_i of total W, set
 c_T=2 sum_{i in T}p_i-W, and S_p(H)=sum_{T in H}c_T.
If S_p(G)>=0 and S_p(H)>=0 for every permissible fibre, summing fibres proves
an abundant coordinate inside A. Uniform weights give
 S(H)=2 sum_{T in H}|T|-n|H|.

For any T in H, G-stability forces I_T={g union T:g in G} into H. Exclude T if
                 2Q(G,I_T)+Q(I_T,I_T)>q.                     (2)
Enlarge the feasible class to all R containing A, excluding those traces, and
obeying the unary implications T -> T union g. Do not impose binary union
closure or the full cost bound (1). A nonnegative minimum over this larger
class is sufficient. A negative minimum need not be a permissible fibre.

Network: one vertex per trace plus source s and sink t. Add s->T of capacity
-c_T for negative c_T, and T->t of capacity c_T for positive c_T. Give every
unary implication capacity M=1+sum |c_T|. Force A using s->A of capacity M;
exclude forbidden traces using T->t of capacity M. A is allowed in all current
tests; otherwise no exterior fibre can exist and that case is handled directly.
Every finite feasible cut has capacity
                 sum_{c_T<0}(-c_T) + S_p(R).                  (3)
A feasible integer flow and a cut of the same capacity certify the exact
minimum. This is an application of the standard closure/min-cut construction;
the generic optimization tool is not a Strange Seed invention.

STRUCTURAL BOUNDARY: A GENUINE NEGATIVE PERMISSIBLE FIBRE

Take five disjoint nonempty coordinate blocks P,Q,R,D,E, and one exterior
coordinate z. Put
 K={empty, P, Q, P union Q, P union Q union R},
 G={k union d union e: k in K, d in {empty,D}, e in {empty,E}},
 H={k union D union E:k in K},
 F=G union {h union {z}:h in H},
 A=P union Q union R union D union E.

Block sizes can vary; the abstract union table stays the same. |G|=20, |H|=5,
|F|=25. All families are union-closed, H is G-stable, and H contains A.
Direct ordered pair counting gives
 Q(G,G)=81, Q(G,H)=36, Q(H,H)=9,
                 2*36+9=81.                                 (4)
Thus H is genuinely permissible, at equality in the modal bound.

Why A is actually modal in F, rather than merely satisfying the necessary
bound: the first factor K has union multiplicities 1,3,3,9,9. The second factor
J={empty,D,E,D union E,D union E union {z}} has the same table. F is their
direct product on disjoint blocks. Ordered union multiplicities multiply, so
the global maximum is 9*9=81, attained by A and by A union {z}, among ties.

Coordinate surpluses within H, per coordinate in each block, are respectively
                     P:+1, Q:+1, R:-3, D:+5, E:+5.
Consequently its uniform surplus is
                     |P|+|Q|-3|R|+5|D|+5|E|.                 (5)
This can be arbitrarily negative by enlarging R. Uniform weighting cannot
possibly be a general certificate for the permissible-fibre condition.

Concrete small version: block sizes (1,1,5,1,1), so |A|=9. Using bit positions
P=0, Q=1, R=2..6, D=7, E=8, its exact fibre is
                     H={384,385,386,387,511}.
It has uniform surplus -3. The core surplus is -52 and the total TARGET
surplus in F is -55. Target surplus counts x intersect A, not exterior z.
The uniform graph relaxation has minimum -25; its minimizing relaxed family
need not satisfy (1), while the explicit H above does. These are distinct facts.

The earlier adversarial separator independently rediscovered this exact H:
5,756 search nodes, witness score -3, witness cost 81, q=81. It did not time out.
This is the single separator call used in this session; the conditional sampled
negative-case calls were unnecessary because all 48 sampled cases passed.

A point weighting on the P coordinate gives an exact graph minimum 0 and core
surplus 4 for this small core. This repair is EASY: G contains the singleton P,
so the familiar injection argument already makes P abundant in any extension.
Do not present this as a novel hard weighted repair.

To remove singleton and two-element sets, take block sizes (3,3,13,3,3).
Now |A|=25, but |G|=20, |H|=5, |F|=25 and all union counts in (4) are unchanged.
The minimum size of a nonempty member of F is 3. H still has surplus -3;
G has surplus -132; the target surplus over F is -135. This version was checked
directly as a 25-set family, WITHOUT constructing a graph on 2^25 traces.
No all-fibres weighted certificate is claimed for this larger core.

In BOTH versions, each target coordinate in P,Q,D,E occurs in 15 of the 25
global sets, while each coordinate in R occurs in 5. Thus the original modal-
support claim holds in these examples. The uniform strengthening fails because
it counts many interchangeable rare coordinates heavily, not because there is
no abundant coordinate. This is the structural lesson of the boundary.

Coordinate symmetry may reduce the weight search: coordinates with identical
membership signatures in G can be permuted without changing the feasible
fibre class. Averaging a valid weight vector over those permutations remains
valid, so weights may be taken equal within each signature block. This is a
standard convexity/symmetry argument, not a novelty claim. It does NOT mean
future exterior fibres must treat a block as an indivisible object: they can
contain partial blocks, and those fibres still need checking. Blindly collapsing
blocks would weaken the audit improperly.
