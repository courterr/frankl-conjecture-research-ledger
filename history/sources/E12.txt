SETUP AND ORIGINAL QUESTION

F is a finite family of distinct sets, closed under union, containing empty.
For A in F, q_F(A) counts ORDERED pairs (B,C) in F squared with B union C=A,
including equal pairs. The original candidate says that every maximizer A of
q_F, including ties, contains a coordinate in at least half of F. We have not
proved this general statement. The previously verified at-most-five target
result is unchanged.

Fix A, n=|A|. Write G={B in F: B subset A}. For any occurring nonempty exterior
trace E, put H_E={B intersect A: B in F and B minus A=E}. Each H contains A,
is union-closed, and is stable under union with G. Define Q(K,L) as the number
of ordered pairs from K times L whose union is A, and q=Q(G,G).

If A is modal, every such H satisfies
                  2 Q(G,H) + Q(H,H) <= q.                     (1)
Indeed these are disjoint contributions to q_F(A union E), whereas q_F(A)=q.
Permissible H means a nonempty family satisfying these necessary conditions.
Different permissible fibres need not coexist in one global family: the test
is deliberately a relaxation, and proving it sufficient is safe.

For nonnegative integer weights p_i, W=sum p_i, define
 c_T=2 sum_{i in T}p_i-W, and S_p(H)=sum_{T in H} c_T.
If S_p(G)>=0 and S_p(H)>=0 for every permissible H, summing over the disjoint
fibres gives some abundant coordinate in A. Uniform p_i=1 makes
 S(H)=2 sum_{T in H}|T|-n|H|.

PROOF COMPRESSION: FORCED INCLUSIONS AND A CLOSURE FLOW

For each possible trace T subset A, G-stability forces its principal image
                    I_T={T union g: g in G}
into H whenever T is in H. Therefore T is forbidden if
                    2Q(G,I_T)+Q(I_T,I_T)>q.                   (2)
This uses monotonicity of pair counts under inclusion of families.

Now enlarge the class of permissible fibres to all R subset 2^A satisfying:
 (a) A in R;
 (b) no forbidden trace in R;
 (c) T in R implies T union g in R for each g in G.
Do NOT impose binary union closure on R, or the full cost bound (1). Every
permissible H is still in this larger class. A nonnegative minimum of S_p(R)
therefore proves a nonnegative minimum for all permissible H.

Build a directed network with one vertex per trace, source s and sink t.
For c_T<0 add s->T of capacity -c_T; for c_T>0 add T->t of capacity c_T.
For each nontrivial implication T->T union g add an edge of capacity M.
Force A by s->A of capacity M, and exclude forbidden T by T->t of capacity M.
Take M=1+sum_T |c_T|. When A is not forbidden, the family {A} is a feasible
finite cut, so an optimal cut cannot cross an M-edge.

The source-side trace set of any such cut is a feasible R, and its capacity is
                 sum_{c_T<0}(-c_T) + S_p(R).                  (3)
A feasible integer flow and a cut with the SAME capacity certify optimality:
every cut is at least the flow value, and this cut attains that value.
Thus verification needs no trust in the optimizing program's termination claim.
If A itself is forbidden, there can be no exterior fibre, and that case can
instead be handled directly; the present flow implementation assumes it is not.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS

verify_flow.py reconstructs each network independently from G and the weights,
recomputes the forbidden traces by direct pair counting, checks every nonzero
flow against capacity, checks flow conservation, computes the cut directly,
and checks the objective identity. It does not call the optimizer to verify the
three exported certificates. All three passed.

For the encoding controls, the optimizer was separately compared against brute
force over every relaxed family R for every eligible three-coordinate core,
under two weightings: 58 comparisons, all matching exactly. Seven additional
single-coordinate weight controls on the selected seven-coordinate core all
returned negative relaxed optima; each exported flow/cut was checked. These
confirm that the method can report failure of a certificate. A negative relaxed
optimum need not be a permissible fibre or disprove the underlying modal claim.

The enumeration and flow implementations are independent in their algorithms,
but were written within this same investigation. This is not external review
or a proof-assistant formalization. The graph-cut reduction and weighted
certificate/cutting-plane methods are established optimization tools. This
report claims neither their invention nor historical novelty of this particular
modal application. Pulaj's 2017 dissertation documents the established weighted
and cutting-plane framework:
https://d-nb.info/1156012813/34

No new general seven-, eight-, or nine-coordinate theorem has been obtained.
The general modal-support candidate remains unresolved. These cores were
selected from prior samples and are not a random efficacy trial of poetry.
