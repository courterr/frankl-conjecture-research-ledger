STRANGE SEED — FRANKL EXPLORATION 23
22 October 2026 (America/Los_Angeles)
Truth & Wonder

OUTCOME
The internally most-frequent-pair LOCAL CERTIFICATE rule does not extend to all target sizes. There is an explicit seven-coordinate core with distinct coordinate-incidence patterns and a unique most-frequent pair for which no normalized nonnegative weighting supported only on that pair certifies every permitted fibre.

A different equal-weight pair certifies all1,513 permitted fibres. Two different complete enumerators agree on every fibre, and Python checks every structural condition and exact margin. Thus this is a counterexample to a shortcut for selecting a local certificate, while the broader two-coordinate weighting strategy survives this example. It does not refute the underlying abundance conjecture, or prove that the most-frequent pair lacks an abundant point globally.

We also obtain a general cloning obstruction to any fixed top-k-frequency local-support rule, plus an exact constructive move for engineering admissible test instances while keeping a negative witness unchanged. No new poem was drawn; this work develops and challenges the existing branch. Poetry's causal benefit and historical novelty are not established.

SETUP AND THE CLAIM ACTUALLY TESTED
F is a finite union-closed family containing the empty set. Let q_F(T) count ordered pairs with union T, r_F(T) count members contained in T, and
 e_F(T)=(q_F(T)-2r_F(T)+1)/2.
This is the number of unordered proper union decompositions of T. The unresolved abundance candidate says every positive global maximizer A of e_F, including tied maximizers, contains an element appearing in at least half of F.

For the core G=F intersect2^A, an occurring exterior trace Z gives H={B intersect A:B in F, B outside A=Z}. Each H is nonempty, contains A, is union-closed, and is G-stable. Put Q(X,Y)=number of ordered pairs with union A and
 C_G(H)=2Q(G,H)+Q(H,H).
A globally e-maximal A forces the adjusted bound
 C_G(H)-2|H| <= q_G(A).
This is derived by counting proper decompositions of A union Z in the core-plus-one-fibre subfamily, and comparing its score with e_F(A).

Write s_i(K)=2*f_K(i)-|K|. A normalized weight vector w>=0 is a sufficient local certificate if sum_i w_i*s_i(K)>=0 for K=G and every permitted H. Summing actual fibres yields global abundance somewhere in the weight support. Failure of such a certificate need not mean the global abundance conclusion is false; it can mean this sufficient proof condition is too strong.

Exploration22 had found that every pair maximizing the sum of two INTERNAL coordinate frequencies certified all fibres in each of1,533 tested six-coordinate cases (748 types). That was exploratory finite evidence. Here we test the proposed extension, rather than claiming that finite result established a theorem in all dimensions.

EXPLICIT SEVEN-COORDINATE CORE
Use zero-based bit coordinates0,...,6, with A=127.
G=[0,45,60,61,63,83,86,87,91,94,95,106,107,110,111,115,119,123,126,127].
There are20 members. Coordinate frequencies are
 [13,16,13,14,14,13,15].
Thus bits1 and6 form the UNIQUE pair of greatest total internal frequency. All seven coordinate-incidence columns are distinct. In particular, the construction is not merely a pair of identical columns or an ambiguity in tie-breaking. The columns for bits1 and6 are still correlated: bit6's core column is strictly contained in bit1's. Distinctness does not imply independence.

q_G(A)=219 and e_G(A)=(219-40+1)/2=90, positive and maximal within G.

EXACT TWO-FIBRE OBSTRUCTION TO ALL WEIGHTS ON THE TOP PAIR
First permitted fibre:
 H1=[13,29,45,61,63,95,111,127].
It has8 members, adjusted cost217<=219, and surpluses on bits(1,6) equal(0,-2).

Second permitted fibre:
 H2=[29,61,63,93,95,125,127].
It has7 members, adjusted cost205<=219, and surpluses on bits(1,6) equal(-1,+1).

Both are union-closed, G-stable and contain A. For nonnegative weights(a,b) supported on bits(1,6), H1 demands -2b>=0, hence b=0. H2 then demands -a>=0, hence a=0. Normalization a+b=1 is impossible. Equivalently, the average of the two surplus vectors is(-1/2,-1/2), an exact negative mixture on both support coordinates. This is a short hand-checkable obstruction after the displayed set arithmetic is verified.

The two fibres are alternative constraints of the local-certificate problem. This argument does NOT require or assert that both occur together in a global e-modal family. Nevertheless, each is separately realizable with the target uniquely maximizing e, so neither is dismissed merely as an impossible one-fibre extension:

For H1, F1=G union {h union {7}:h in H1} has28 members, coordinate frequencies [21,20,21,22,19,18,18], and A is the UNIQUE e-maximizer at90.
For H2, F2 has27 members, frequencies [20,19,20,21,21,17,19], and A is likewise the UNIQUE e-maximizer at90.
Every target coordinate is abundant in each of these two actual families. They are not counterexamples to Frankl's conjecture or to our e-mode abundance claim.

WHAT WORKS INSTEAD
Complete enumeration yields1,513 permitted H. Every unit-coordinate local certificate fails: exact minimum fibre surpluses for coordinates0,...,6 are
 [-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-2].
However the equal-weight pair(0,2) has combined core surplus12 and minimum combined fibre surplus0. Hence for ANY actual global F with this fixed core and globally e-maximal A,
 2*f_F(0)+2*f_F(2)-2*|F|>=12,
so
 f_F(0)+f_F(2)>=|F|+6.
At least one of these coordinates is globally abundant, with frequency at least ceil((|F|+6)/2). There is no restriction on how many exterior coordinates or fibres F may have.

This establishes a computer-assisted restricted result for this core, not a universal seven-coordinate theorem. Fifteen of the21 equal-weight pairs work; the selected top pair is among the failures. Thus neither 'no single coordinate works' nor 'the highest-frequency pair fails' implies that support size greater than two is necessary.

GENERAL OBSTRUCTION TO FIXED TOP-k-FREQUENCY SUPPORT
A six-coordinate seed is
 G0=[0,19,22,23,27,31,42,43,44,45,46,47,51,55,59,60,61,62,63],
 H0=[13,29,31,45,47,61,63].
Its internal frequencies are[12,14,12,13,12,13], so bit1 is uniquely most frequent. Yet bit1 appears in only3 of the7 H0 members, giving surplus-1. The seed has q_G0=189, e_G0(top)=76, and adjusted cost173<=189.

For any integer k>=1, replace bit1 with k coordinates that are present together exactly when bit1 was present, retaining the other five coordinates. This map on sets is injective and preserves unions, subset relations and family cardinalities. Consequently all union-pair counts, r values, proper-decomposition scores and the adjusted cost are preserved. Union closure and G stability are also preserved.

The k clone coordinates each have internal frequency14; every other coordinate has frequency at most13. Thus they are exactly the top k coordinates, without a frequency tie at the cutoff. Each has surplus-1 in the copied H0. Every normalized nonnegative weight vector supported on those top k therefore has fibre surplus-1. No such local certificate exists, regardless of the fixed k selected. The target has dimension k+5.

This is a general mathematical obstruction from an explicit verified seed, not an inference from testing a few k values. The verifier's k=1,2,3,8 examples are sanity checks of the implementation. It rules out the literal frequency-ranking local-certificate recipe on arbitrary coordinate representations. It does not rule out choosing other support coordinates, or selection rules that account for membership structure. The separate seven-coordinate construction above shows that merely eliminating identical coordinate columns is insufficient to rescue the top-two rule.

HOW THE SEPARATING COUNTEREXAMPLE WAS FOUND
This was an adaptive mathematical construction, not a blind comparative trial.
1. Clone the seed's bad highest-frequency coordinate, producing a strict top-two frequency pair and a negative copied fibre. This already refutes the literal rule, but the two columns coincide.
2. Freeze and test all64 single added traces containing exactly one of those two bits, closing the core and the copied fibre under required unions. None retained the targeted pair(1,6) as the unique top pair while satisfying the separating-core, internal-mode and adjusted-cost requirements for that negative witness. Some did give tied-pair failures.
3. Three closed refinements that added both separating coatoms and removed one of the original nonclone traces gave a unique pair but the retained witness exceeded the cap. Exact separator runs completed for those cores and certified the pair there; those variants were not accepted as counterexamples.
4. The more targeted core (cloned G0 minus44) union{63}, with the corresponding8-member H1, had distinct columns and a unique top pair but q=201 versus adjusted cost207. This near-miss was explicitly rejected. Its one-fibre extension actually had exterior-top e=85 greater than target e=82, confirming why the cost failure matters.
5. Preserve H1 and search its stabilizer: traces t satisfying t union h in H1 for every h in H1. Adjoining such a t can change the core without changing H1. Restrict candidates to traces containing both selected bits, protecting their frequency ranking. Of the24 such stabilizer traces,10 were missing from the near-miss core. Test each missing trace followed by core closure, and their total completion. Four constructions passed the specified local conditions. The selected minimum-size one adjoins94, giving the displayed20-member core.

Exact cost improvement for the final step:
94 has9 union-to-top partners in the near-miss core but only5 in H1. Adding94 preserves union closure without further core additions and preserves H1's G stability. It raises q_G by18, from201 to219, but raises the adjusted witness cost by only10, from207 to217. The slack improves by8, changing an inadmissible deficit6 into an admissible margin2. Both internal and actual-global e-modal maximality were then checked separately.

REUSABLE CONSTRUCTIVE MOVE
For a fixed negative H, consider S_H={t:t union h in H for all h in H}. S_H is union-closed. If t is adjoined to a core while retaining closure and t!=A, then
 delta q_G=2*Q({t},G),
 delta[C_G(H)-2|H|]=2*Q({t},H).
Thus the admissibility slack q_G-[C_G(H)-2|H|] improves by
 2*[Q({t},G)-Q({t},H)].
Searching for stabilizing traces with more core union partners than fibre union partners can preserve a failure witness while repairing its cost admissibility. Closure additions, coordinate-frequency ranking and internal/global modal status still require checking; the formula alone does not guarantee them. This move worked here and is a concrete candidate for future adversarial instance construction, without any claim of historical originality or evidence that poetry caused it.

VERIFICATION
Primary enumeration: canonical incremental union closure, adjusted cost and valid cardinality bound |H|<=floor((q_G-2|G|+3)/2)=91. Negative-score early exit was disabled to enumerate the entire permitted class. Completed14,679 nodes and1,513 fibres.
Independent enumeration: include/exclude traversal over128 possible traces, iterative closure propagation, additive adjusted cost (subtract2 for each new trace), root adjusted cost2|G|-1. With uniform coordinate weights, the routine could stop on a negative total surplus; it did not. It completed16,191 nodes and1,513 fibres, minimum uniform surplus7.
Both had two-million-node and three-second limits; no limit was reached. Their full hexadecimal family-mask sets agree exactly. Python independently checked every emitted fibre for union closure, G stability, top membership and adjusted cost; recomputed every coordinate/pair margin; checked the two displayed global extensions by direct proper-decomposition pair counting; and checked distinct core columns, unique frequency ranking and all accepted certificate inequalities.

Adjusted-cost pruning is safe: adding a trace to a family containing A adds at least2 cross-pair contributions with the core's A, at least2 ordered H-H contributions with A, and subtracts2 for the new trace. Its adjusted cost therefore increases by at least2, so a pruned family cannot later return below the cap. The cardinality bound follows from Q(G,H)>=|G|+|H|-1 and Q(H,H)>=2|H|-1.

This is computer-assisted verification, not formal proof-assistant certification or external review. No literature search was performed in this session, and historical novelty is unconfirmed.

DECISION AND NEXT COURSE
Retire the universal internally-most-frequent-pair local-certificate shortcut. It may remain a heuristic, but it cannot replace checking all permitted fibres. The broad weighted strategy survives, and the challenge of finding a core defeating every one- and two-coordinate support remains open in this project.
A productive next step is to reuse witness-preserving stabilizer additions to target simultaneous obstructions across all support pairs, with exact infeasibility witnesses and strict budgets. The successful counterexample does not justify claiming a route to the full abundance conjecture has been found. The useful progress is a falsified shortcut, an explicit repair, and a concrete way to generate sharper adversarial instances.
