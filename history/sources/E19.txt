STRANGE SEED — FRANKL EXPLORATION 19
21 October 2026 (America/Los_Angeles)
Truth & Wonder

RESULT
The known six-coordinate core that defeats every single-coordinate local certificate still admits a two-coordinate certificate for the new proper-decomposition score. All 427 permitted exterior fibres satisfy the equal-weight certificate on coordinates 4 and 5 (zero-based bit indices). The previous q-score permitted only 316 of them. The 111 extra fibres introduce no failure of these weights.

This is a computer-assisted restricted theorem for this fixed internal core and arbitrary ambient size, not a proof of the general conjecture. It does not measure whether poetry improves discovery, nor establish historical novelty. No new poem was drawn in this continuation.

DEFINITIONS AND FIXED INSTANCE
F is a finite union-closed family containing the empty set. For T in F, q_F(T) counts ordered pairs with union T, and r_F(T) counts family members contained in T. Define e_F(T)=(q_F(T)-2r_F(T)+1)/2. Equivalently, e counts unordered pairs of proper subsets in F whose union is T.
The candidate under investigation is: every positive global maximizer A of e contains a coordinate belonging to at least half of F.

Identify A with the six-bit set 63. The fixed internal core is
G=[0,19,22,23,27,31,42,43,44,45,46,47,51,55,59,60,61,62,63].
It has 19 members, q_G(A)=189, e_G(A)=76, and coordinate frequencies [12,14,12,13,12,13]. Bits 4 and 5 therefore have combined core surplus (2*12-19)+(2*13-19)=12.

Each occurring nonempty exterior trace Z gives H={B intersect A:B in F and B outside A=Z}. H is nonempty, contains A, is union-closed, and is stable under union with G. Put Q(X,Y)=number of ordered pairs (x,y) in X times Y with x union y=A, and C(H)=2Q(G,H)+Q(H,H).
The correct necessary cap for an e-maximizing A is C(H)-2|H| <=189. It is not the older stronger cap C(H)<=189.

WHY THE CAP IS NECESSARY
For D=A union Z, restrict attention to the subfamily G together with the Z-fibre. The ordered union pairs producing D are precisely the two cross orders plus the H-H pairs, totaling C(H). Among them, exactly 2(19+|H|)-1 involve D itself. Thus this subfamily already contributes [C(H)-2(19+|H|)+1]/2 proper decompositions of D. Full F can only add more. Since e_F(A)=76 and A maximizes e_F, the claimed adjusted cap follows.

EXACT CERTIFICATE AND GLOBAL CONSEQUENCE
For every one of the 427 admissible H, verified exactly:
  (2*f_H(4)-|H|)+(2*f_H(5)-|H|) >=0.
The minimum is zero. In the core, the same expression is 12. Summing over the core and every exterior fibre gives
  2*f_F(4)+2*f_F(5)-2*|F| >=12,
so
  f_F(4)+f_F(5) >= |F|+6.
In particular, at least one of these two coordinates has frequency at least ceil((|F|+6)/2). Thus the candidate holds whenever the internal core of the target is a coordinate permutation of this G. The result permits any number of exterior coordinates and fibres; it does not require independently permitted fibres to coexist.

ENUMERATION AND VERIFICATION
First enumeration: canonical closure traversal in C++, starting at {63}, adding a trace and closing under unions with the existing family and G. Canonical lower-bit checks suppress duplicates. The adjusted cost is monotone during additions to a family containing 63: adding h !=63 adds at least 2 from cross pairs with g=63, at least 2 ordered H-H pairs with 63, and subtracts 2 for cardinality, for a net increase of at least 2. Cost pruning is therefore sound. The finite closure traversal completed at 2,838 visited nodes, below the frozen five-million-node budget, returning 427 distinct fibres. The outer 20-second process limit was not reached.
Second enumeration: Python include/exclude traversal over all 64 possible traces, iterative union closure, exact integer adjusted-cost checks and a five-million-node budget. It completed at 5,675 nodes. Its entire set of 427 family masks exactly equals the first enumerator's output, not merely its count.
Every emitted family was separately checked for containing 63, union closure, G stability, and the adjusted bound. The original C++ unrestricted four-coordinate control still returns 4,542 families containing the top.
All 427 actual one-exterior-coordinate extensions F=G union {h union {6}:h in H} were also checked directly. In every case A remains a global e-maximizer, and none has every target coordinate rare. This is supplementary verification of a finite family class; the certificate above is what establishes the unrestricted exterior-size conclusion.

An optional linear program also found the different rational weights [0,0,1/10,0,4/5,1/10], verified exactly after reconstruction. These are unnecessary for the principal result: the simpler old weights [0,0,0,0,1,1] already work. No claim rests on floating-point feasibility alone.

LIMITS AND NEXT DECISION
We have a complete restricted certificate for one previously obstructive six-coordinate core. We have not classified all six-coordinate cores, ruled out a general counterexample, or proved that a common weighting always exists. The size-five result from Exploration 18 stands separately. A substantive next challenge would be a different six-coordinate core whose adjusted-cap fibres defeat simple weightings, with the same distinction between a failed certificate and a failed mathematical claim. There is no need to redraw a poem just to verify the current branch.
