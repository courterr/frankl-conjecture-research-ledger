PRECISE CANDIDATE

Let F be a finite union-closed family of distinct sets, containing empty and
at least one nonempty member. For A in F put
 r_F(A)=|{B in F:B subset A}|,
 q_F(A)=#{(B,C) in F squared:B union C=A}, ordered pairs including equal pairs.

Define e_F(A) as the number of UNORDERED pairs {B,C} of proper members below A
such that B union C=A. The two members are distinct and incomparable. Exactly
2r_F(A)-1 ordered pairs involve A itself. Every remaining ordered pair has two
distinct proper members and occurs in both orders. Consequently
                    e_F(A)=(q_F(A)-2r_F(A)+1)/2.             (1)

Candidate: if max_A e_F(A)>0, EVERY maximizing A, including ties, contains a
coordinate occurring in at least half of the GLOBAL family F.
The conclusion is not that every coordinate in A is abundant.

If max e=0, F is a chain: any incomparable pair would give a positive proper
decomposition of its union. Conversely a chain has no such pair. In a finite
nontrivial chain, every coordinate of its least nonempty member occurs in all
|F|-1 nonempty members, so is abundant. Thus the general candidate, if proved,
would imply Frankl's conjecture; families omitting empty can first have it added.
This implication is an observation, not a proof
of the candidate or of Frankl.

The score is different from q-mode selection. For example, start with the eight
subsets of {a,b,c}, then append seven successively larger greatest members, each
adding a fresh coordinate. The old target {a,b,c} has q=27 and e=6. Each new
greatest member has e=0, since it introduces a coordinate absent below it; the
last has q=29 and becomes the q-mode. Removing automatic pairs therefore changes
the selector in a concrete way. No implication between the two general modal
claims has been established.

HAND PROOF: COATOM-ONLY INTERNAL CORE

Suppose A maximizes a positive e score and its internal core has the form
 G={empty,A} union {A minus {i}:i in I}, with t=|I|.
There are exactly binomial(t,2) proper decompositions of A, one for each distinct
pair of its internal coatoms. Positivity implies t>=2, and
                         e_F(A)=t(t-1)/2.                   (2)

Take any occurring nonempty exterior trace Z, and its fibre
 H={B intersect A:B in F and B minus A=Z}.
It contains A and is G-stable. Let C={j in I:A minus {j} belongs to H}, c=|C|.

For each j in C, the exterior set (A minus {j}) union Z is a proper subset of
D=A union Z. Its union with the internal member A gives D. So does its union
with each of the t-1 internal coatoms A minus {i}, i != j. These are t distinct
proper unordered decomposition pairs for D, one member internal, one exterior.
Different j give different pairs. Hence e_F(D)>=ct.

Modal maximality and (2) give
                         ct<=t(t-1)/2,
so c< t/2. If T in H omits some i in I, G-stability forces
 (A minus {i}) union T=A minus {i}
into H. Therefore every omitted i belongs to C. Each T contains at least t-c
elements of I, strictly more than half of I.

Within the internal core, the total I incidence is t^2: each of t coatoms has
t-1 members of I, A has t, and empty has zero. Its |G|=t+2 sets therefore have
surplus 2t^2-t(t+2)=t(t-2)>=0 relative to half of I. Every exterior fibre has
strictly positive I surplus by the previous paragraph. Summing all disjoint
fibres gives average global frequency at least half among the coordinates I.
Thus some coordinate of I, and hence of A, is globally abundant.

This proof permits arbitrary target size, ambient universe and number of
exterior fibres. It uses e-mode maximality, not q-mode maximality. It resembles
the earlier coatom argument structurally, but its decisive proper-pair lower
bound is ct; that bound has been derived explicitly for the new score.

COROLLARY: EVERY TARGET OF SIZE AT MOST FOUR

If F contains a singleton {x} subset A, union with {x} injects sets not containing
x into those containing x, so x is abundant.

If F contains a two-element set {x,y} subset A, partition F by presence/absence
of x,y, with counts n00,n10,n01,n11. Union with {x,y} injects the 00 class into
the 11 class, so n11>=n00. Hence f(x)+f(y)=n10+n01+2n11>=|F|, and at least one
is abundant. These elementary arguments do not require modal maximality.

For |A|<=4, if no nonempty member of the internal core has size at most two,
the only possible proper nonempty members are three-element coatoms when
|A|=4. A positive e score requires at least two such coatoms. The preceding
lemma applies. For smaller A, a positive score already requires an internal
singleton or pair. Therefore the candidate holds for every target of size at
most four, with UNBOUNDED ambient universe. This is stronger than merely
checking families whose entire universe has size four.
