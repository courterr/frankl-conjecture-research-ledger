## Established overlap to keep visible

- Local configurations, restricted fibres, missing traces and largest-disjoint
  anchors: documented historical sources include Pulaj/Wood and Gao/Yu.
- Weighted coordinate certificates, exact feasibility and cutting planes:
  Poonen and Pulaj; not invented by this project.
- Inclusion-respecting matching as a universal route: historical counterexamples
  already existed in Polymath2016. Our finite-geometry output is not a priority
  claim for the basic impossibility of that route.
- Union-infinite divisibility: directly matched for the probabilistic-root seed.
- Generators, irredundant covers, private coordinates, graph closure/min-cut,
  symmetry and coordinate cloning: established ingredients.
- Uniform union sampling and entropy: established in the Gilmer/Sawin direction;
  the project's modal selectors are specific additional conjectures.

No fresh comprehensive literature search was performed while compiling v0.5.
Earlier source inspections are reported as historical inspections, not silently
represented as newly repeated or exhaustive. The exact selectors, restricted
results and examples retain unconfirmed priority.

## Rule for future work

Before substantial work on a newly claimed approach, record the precise claim,
closest prior results, inspected sections, queries/date, match or uncertainty,
and a decision to proceed, cite, modify or stop. Keep novelty relative to the
baseline separate from novelty relative to literature, mathematical validity
and usefulness. Missing timing stays undocumented unless a source fills it.
