## Still unresolved in this project

- The general q-mode selector and the general positive-e-mode selector.
- A complete global transfer from the earlier triple/anchor branch.
- Whether all modal cores admit some local coordinate weighting.
- Whether an instance can defeat every support of size at most two in the
  adjusted local program; none was constructed here.
- Whether the particular restricted results or examples are historically new.

These open items are not made more feasible merely by counting previous
partial gains. A new attempt should name which exact obstruction or missing
hypothesis it changes.
