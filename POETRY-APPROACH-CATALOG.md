# Poetry-associated approaches and their stopping points

Expanded handoff v0.5 — 23 October 2026. This is an account of the Frankl work
documented in this conversation's saved records. It includes proposals made in
poetry-conditioned runs and their subsequent mathematical follow-through.
It does not label all descendants as fresh poetry discoveries. Baseline/control
work appears only to identify duplicates, origins or relevant counterexamples.

The source audit covers one baseline, three pilots and Explorations01–25.
Eight recorded draws selected seven distinct poem titles; the repeated Blake
draw was retained. The number of attempts below is an editorial breakdown of
claims and subroutes, not an efficacy denominator or count of novel discoveries.

**The research time-saving unit is a precise claim with a result and boundary.**
A failed local sufficient certificate is not a counterexample to Frankl.
A bounded search finding nothing is not a proof of impossibility. A valid
restricted lemma can still leave the original problem untouched. Novelty and
the causal benefit of poetry remain unconfirmed.

See [SOURCE-COVERAGE.md](SOURCE-COVERAGE.md) for the source reconciliation,
[SEED-LINEAGE.md](SEED-LINEAGE.md) for origins, and
[VERIFICATION-SCOPE.md](VERIFICATION-SCOPE.md) for what is freshly checked.

### P01-C1 — Classify negative triple fibres

**Lineage:** S01; poetry-conditioned proposal; baseline overlap. **Outcome:** valid restricted result; duplicated by controls.

**Proposal:** Classify all union-closed trace families on a three-element member and use their deficits to force abundance.

**Attempt:** The seeded condition classified the negative cases; all three conditions did so independently. The parent checked all 128 top-containing candidate families.

**Result:** There are 90 union-closed top-containing fibres. Six labelled fibres, in two permutation types, have negative balance, each exactly -1.

**Boundary:** Classification is not a global abundance argument, and was already accessible from the baseline decomposition.

**Avoid repeating:** Do not claim this shared classification as a poem-specific discovery. A global proof needs control of how many deficits occur.

**Verification in this release:** verify_history.py: triple-fibre classification

**Sources:** [BASE](history/sources/BASE.txt), [P01](history/sources/P01.txt).

<a id="p01-c2"></a>

### P01-C2 — Parity-sensitive deficit tolerance

**Lineage:** S01; poetry-conditioned proposal; control overlap. **Outcome:** valid restricted result; no general bridge.

**Proposal:** Retain integer frequencies instead of treating the three-element balance as a continuous average.

**Attempt:** Let b count negative fibres and p their total positive compensation. Compare total balance p-b with the largest possible value when all three coordinates are rare.

**Result:** All-three rarity forces total balance <=-3 for odd m and <=-6 for even m. Hence b-p<3 or <6 respectively suffices for an abundant triple coordinate.

**Boundary:** This supplies a conditional test, not a way to guarantee its hypothesis in an arbitrary family. Integrality also appeared in a control.

**Avoid repeating:** An inequality with a strict threshold cannot be relaxed to equality without another argument.

**Verification in this release:** historical report only

**Sources:** [P01](history/sources/P01.txt).

<a id="p01-c3"></a>

### P01-C3 — Nontrivial union coupling with the original law

**Lineage:** S01; poetry-conditioned proposal. **Outcome:** mechanism blocked.

**Proposal:** Couple two copies of the uniform family law so their union retains that same law, hoping for a nontrivial entropy route.

**Attempt:** Use containment and the identity E|B union C|-E|B|=E|B symmetric-difference C|/2 for equal input laws on a finite universe.

**Result:** Exact preservation forces B=C almost surely. A nontrivial union coupling cannot preserve the full input law. A total-variation lower bound was also derived.

**Boundary:** This blocks that exact-preservation mechanism, not approximate couplings or the established entropy program.

**Avoid repeating:** Any repair must explicitly relax distribution preservation and control the resulting error.

**Verification in this release:** historical report only

**Sources:** [P01](history/sources/P01.txt), [E01](history/sources/E01.txt).

### P02-C1 — Projective-plane obstruction to universal inclusion matching

**Lineage:** S02; poetry-conditioned proposal. **Outcome:** stronger certificate refuted; historical overlap.

**Proposal:** Use local upward-closed imbalance to test the proposed injection from coordinate-absent sets to supersets containing that coordinate.

**Attempt:** Construct a 29-member complement family from the seven-point projective plane and compute a maximum matching at each coordinate.

**Result:** Every coordinate occurs in 21/29 members, yet each absent side has eight members and maximum inclusion matching seven. No coordinate supplies the proposed certificate.

**Boundary:** This refutes inclusion-respecting matching, not abundance. The universal monotone-matching direction already had a 2016 counterexample, identified in the later literature correction.

**Avoid repeating:** Do not try to infer an inclusion-respecting injection merely from global abundance; account for every local Hall constraint.

**Verification in this release:** verify_history.py: explicit 29-set construction and all seven matchings

**Sources:** [P02](history/sources/P02.txt), [P03](history/sources/P03.txt).

### P02-C2 — Allow a fixed unmatched-source budget

**Lineage:** S02; poetry-conditioned generalization plus ordinary follow-up. **Outcome:** proposed repair refuted.

**Proposal:** Repair universal inclusion matching by allowing at most a fixed number of unmatched sources.

**Attempt:** Generalize the complement construction using projective planes of prime order q. Pilot03 ordinary follow-up supplied an exact maximum-matching count.

**Result:** There are 1+(q^2+q+1)2^q sets; the unmatched deficit is 2^q-q-1, unbounded, while coordinate abundance tends to one.

**Boundary:** The general construction and formulas are historical proof claims in the source excerpts; v0.5 directly checks q=2 only. The exact-count follow-up was ordinary work, not a new poem.

**Avoid repeating:** A universal constant allowance cannot fix the rule. A structural, growing allowance would be a different claim.

**Verification in this release:** historical report only

**Sources:** [P02](history/sources/P02.txt), [P03](history/sources/P03.txt).

### P03-C1 — Anchor at the actual largest disjoint member

**Lineage:** S03; poetry-conditioned proposal; control duplicate. **Outcome:** valid criterion; duplicated and related to prior literature.

**Proposal:** Replace the whole outside universe by Y, the union of family members actually disjoint from a chosen triple S.

**Attempt:** Use missing singleton traces at Y to obtain a nonnegative balance on the complementary pair.

**Result:** If all three S-elements are rare, the anchor fibre H_Y must be the full triple cube. The structured control independently proved the equivalent statement.

**Boundary:** The full cube alone need not have enough mass to force a globally abundant outside coordinate. Exploration01 identified the broad missing-trace framework as established.

**Avoid repeating:** Do not repeat the anchor criterion as a new global bridge; its remaining difficulty is quantitative transfer.

**Verification in this release:** historical report only

**Sources:** [P03](history/sources/P03.txt), [E01](history/sources/E01.txt).

### P03-C2 — An incomplete terminal fibre forces abundance in S

**Lineage:** S03; poetry-conditioned falsification; control duplicate. **Outcome:** stronger claim refuted.

**Proposal:** Test the earlier unresolved assertion that an incomplete terminal fibre alone forces an abundant coordinate in the selected triple.

**Attempt:** Append a fresh greatest member to a triple-deficient example.

**Result:** The seeded condition gave a 37-member family with triple frequencies18 and terminal fibre {S}; a structured control gave 35/17. The terminal-only implication is false.

**Boundary:** This does not refute the earlier restricted theorem requiring the whole outside universe to be a member. Controls also found the top-extension mechanism.

**Avoid repeating:** Changing the terminal fibre by a new top layer can preserve the underlying deficiency. The actual anchor carries information that a terminal layer may lose.

**Verification in this release:** historical report only

### E01-L — Localize deficiency inside S union Y

**Lineage:** S04; fresh poem-associated proposal; overlaps pre-draw directions. **Outcome:** proved restricted bound; global application stalled.

**Proposal:** If every element of a triple S is rare, more than 9m/16 family members lie inside S union Y.

**Attempt:** Bound fibre balance by -h/3 generally and by 3h/7 when the empty trace is absent; construct sharp examples.

**Result:** With I=|{A in F:A subset S union Y}|, max_(x in S) f(x)>=(36m-8I)/63. Deficiency forces I>9m/16. A sequence has m=16q-5, I=9q+9 and triple frequencies8q-3.

**Boundary:** An abundant element in the majority subfamily need not reach half the whole family. The constant is asymptotically sharp; simply improving it indefinitely cannot close the gap.

**Avoid repeating:** A successful continuation needs cross-union compatibility or a genuine lift of one common coordinate, not just a majority subfamily.

**Verification in this release:** verify_history.py: local inequalities and sharp examples

**Sources:** [E01](history/sources/E01.txt).

**Lineage:** S04; follow-up. **Outcome:** stronger transfer refuted.

**Proposal:** Assume a coordinate abundant inside the localized core remains globally abundant.

**Attempt:** Recover a 55-member construction: core45 with triple frequencies(23,20,20), exterior pieces of sizes4 and6.

**Boundary:** Other coordinates remain abundant. The example rejects naive lifting, not Frankl or every stronger transfer condition.

**Avoid repeating:** Core abundance requires a quantified exterior margin; it cannot be transferred without loss.

### E02-B — Omission-budget bookkeeping

**Lineage:** S04; follow-up. **Outcome:** identity valid; missing structural inequality.

**Proposal:** For core candidates K subset Y, control dilution by counting exterior omissions.

**Attempt:** Write sigma_K for total core surplus and M_K=sum_(A in exterior)|K minus A|.

**Result:** Exact total surplus is sigma_K+|K|O-2M_K. Nonnegativity is a sufficient certificate. Simultaneous dilution demands the opposite strict inequality.

**Boundary:** The identity itself does not exploit union closure and requires the unknown incidence information. No general upper bound on simultaneous omissions was obtained.

**Avoid repeating:** Restating the desired incidence bound is not a new proof. Supply a structural omission bound before reinvesting.

**Verification in this release:** historical report only

**Sources:** [E02](history/sources/E02.txt), [E03](history/sources/E03.txt).

### E02-C — Depth-at-most-two omission transfer

**Lineage:** S04; follow-up. **Outcome:** valid restricted theorem; extension to three refuted.

**Proposal:** Transfer two core-abundant anchor coordinates when every exterior member omits at most two elements of Y, and try to extend it.

**Attempt:** Pair the possible hole {u,v} with the full completion in each outside part; then build nested layers with holes of size up to three.

**Result:** At depth<=2, f_H(u)+f_H(v)>=|H|. At depth3 a 49-member example has u,v core frequencies8/9 but global frequencies24/49.

**Boundary:** The counterexample has abundant S-elements; it does not settle the additional all-S-rare simultaneous-dilution conjecture.

**Avoid repeating:** The numerical depth hypothesis cannot simply be removed. Keep the all-S-rare condition separate.

**Verification in this release:** verify_history.py: 49-set family and counts
