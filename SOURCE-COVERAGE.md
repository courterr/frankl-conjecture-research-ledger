## Coverage rules

- Every source record maps to at least one catalog entry; the baseline maps
  only as context. Entries can share sources and one source can support several
  subclaims. Record counts must not be confused with independent approach counts.
- Every entry states its proposal, attempt, result, limit and a concrete
  research-time-saving consequence. Positive partial results are retained
  alongside falsifications and incomplete searches.
- Recoverable counterexamples and near misses were selected for fresh finite
  checking; other computational-completeness claims stay explicitly historical.
- A documented literature check is not turned into a novelty certificate.
  Missing check timing is not invented. See NOVELTY-AND-PRIOR-ART.md and
  HISTORICAL-AUDIT.md for the updated distinction.

Machine-readable counterparts: history/attempts.json and
history/source-coverage.json. Check the catalog references and integrity
manifest using verify_release.py.
