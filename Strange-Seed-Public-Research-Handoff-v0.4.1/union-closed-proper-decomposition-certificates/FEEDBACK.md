# Corrections and prior-art requests

We welcome prior-art references, equivalent formulations, proof-gap reports,
and independently reproduced checks. The results are exploratory and
substantially AI-assisted. Novelty is unconfirmed; the historical audit is partial.

Please identify the claim by its ID in claims.json or the section in NOTE.md.

For a prior-art match, provide the reference, theorem/section and the mapping
between its notation and our statement. Distinguish “uses a familiar method”
from “the specific claim is already known or follows immediately.” Either
finding is useful, but they have different implications.

For a proof gap, state the step, missing assumption or concrete failing family.
For a reproduction report, record the package version/commit, Python version,
commands, output and any modifications. Report both successes and failures.

Questions of particular interest:

- Is the positive global proper-decomposition-maximizer selector already known?
- Has the adjusted stable-fibre cap been used in an equivalent formulation?
- Are the explicit 17/4 classification, cost609 minimum or q629 barrier
  subsumed by an existing theorem?

See QUESTIONS.md for additional mathematical questions. No conclusion about
poetry's causal effectiveness follows from this package.
