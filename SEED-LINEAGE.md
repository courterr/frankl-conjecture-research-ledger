## Attribution and rights correction

The frozen collection's P026 metadata named Edward Taylor. Exploration01
corrected the selected work to James Tate's *Dream On* and recorded that it was
copyrighted. The old blanket public-domain assumption about the corpus must
not be reused. This package supplies titles, authors, reported associations
and mathematics, without reproducing the poems or distributing the corpus.

## Attribution is not efficacy

Pilot01's central classification also appeared in both controls. Pilot03's
anchor and terminal-fibre developments were duplicated by controls. Pilot02's
finite-geometry construction appeared in the seeded output in that comparison,
but the universal matching direction already had a historical counterexample.
The later explorations were adaptive, and their descendants were not independent
fresh-poem trials. None of these observations establishes a general poetry effect.
